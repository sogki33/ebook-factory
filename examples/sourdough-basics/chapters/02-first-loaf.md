# Your First Loaf

This chapter turns your active starter into one round loaf over two days. Most of that time the dough is resting; your hands are busy for about **25 minutes in total**.

## The formula

Bakers write recipes as percentages of the flour weight. This simple 70 % hydration dough is forgiving and easy to shape.

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Bread flour | 450 g | 90 % |
| Whole-wheat flour | 50 g | 10 % |
| Water | 350 g | 70 % |
| Active starter | 100 g | 20 % |
| Salt | 10 g | 2 % |

> [!TIP] Write the time on tape
> Stick a piece of tape on the bowl and write the time of each step. Fermentation depends on temperature, and your notes become your own recipe.

## Mix, rest and fold

1. **Mix** flour and 330 g water until no dry flour remains. Rest 45 minutes.
2. **Add** starter, salt and the last 20 g water. Squeeze it through your fingers for 3 minutes.
3. **Fold** every 30 minutes, four times: grab one side, stretch it up, fold it over; turn the bowl and repeat.
4. **Wait** until the dough has grown about 50 %, is domed and jiggles when you shake the bowl. At 24 °C that's roughly 4–5 hours from mixing.

![Stretch-and-fold: lift one edge, fold it across, turn the bowl a quarter](images/shaping.jpg)

> [!DO] Wet hands, not floured hands
> During folding, dip your hand in water. Extra flour now makes a dry, dense crumb later.

## Shape and cold-proof overnight

Tip the dough onto an unfloured counter, fold the edges to the middle and roll it over so the seam is underneath. Drag it toward you a few times to tighten the surface. Place it seam-up in a floured bowl or basket, cover it and refrigerate for 12–16 hours.

> [!WARNING] Over-proofing
> If a floured finger poke stays dented and doesn't spring back at all, the dough is over-proofed. Bake it anyway in a loaf tin — it will still taste good.

## Bake

Put a lidded cast-iron pot in the oven and heat to 250 °C for 45 minutes. Turn the cold dough out onto baking paper, cut one confident slash about 1 cm deep, and lower it into the hot pot.

| Stage | Lid | Temperature | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spring | On | 250 °C | 20 min |
| Colour | Off | 230 °C | 20–25 min |
| Cool | — | Room temp | 1 hour minimum |

![Bake until deeply browned — colour is flavour](images/baked-loaf.jpg)

### Bake-day checklist

- [ ] Oven and pot preheated for 45 minutes
- [ ] Baking paper cut to size
- [ ] Razor or sharp knife ready for scoring
- [ ] Oven gloves within reach
- [ ] Timer set for the lid-off moment

> [!IMPORTANT] Let it cool
> The inside keeps cooking as it cools. Cutting a loaf before one hour makes the crumb gummy, no matter how well you baked it.
